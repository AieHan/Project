❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Does this tool store my passwords?

No! Your passwords are never sent to any server. Everything runs locally in your browser. The history only stores strength scores, not actual passwords.

Is this tool free to use?

Yes, completely free! It's an open-source educational project.

Can I use this for my organization?

Absolutely! Feel free to share it with your team.

How accurate is the strength checker?

The checker follows industry best practices. For maximum security, always combine strong passwords with 2FA.
